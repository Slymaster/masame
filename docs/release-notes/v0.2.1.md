Masame 0.2.1 is a small update for macOS: the app is now signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The "Apple could not verify that Masame is free of malware" warning is gone, and there is no **Open Anyway** step any more.

Nothing else changes. If you have 0.2.0, Masame offers this update itself; click Install.

## Downloads

| File | System |
|---|---|
| `Masame_0.2.1_aarch64.dmg` | macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon only. Intel Macs are not supported yet. |
| `Masame_0.2.1_x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, x64. **Preview:** built and unit-tested by CI, still little tried on real hardware. |
| `Masame_0.2.1_amd64.AppImage`, `Masame_0.2.1_amd64.deb`, `Masame-0.2.1-1.x86_64.rpm` | Linux x86_64 with glibc 2.35 or later (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12, Fedora, Linux Mint 21+). **Preview.** |

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The other files (`latest.json`, `.tar.gz`, `.sig`) are for the in-app updater.

## First launch

- **macOS.** Open the DMG, drag Masame to Applications, then open it. Nothing else to do.
- **Windows.** The installer is not signed yet. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer is per-user and needs no administrator rights.
- **Linux.** The AppImage may need `libfuse2`. With NVIDIA's proprietary driver, a blank window is fixed by starting Masame with `WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=1`. Stone sounds need GStreamer's "good" plugins.
